Privacy Policy — Bybit V1 Backup

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Bybit V1 Backup is a private utility used by its owner to store and retrieve encrypted backup files in the owner's own Google Drive account.

Google account access

The utility uses Google OAuth and the Google Drive API drive.file permission. This is used solely to create, list, read, update, and delete backup files that the application is authorized to access. The utility does not request access to email, contacts, calendars, or the entire Google Drive.

Data processed

When configured, the utility transfers encrypted backup archives and associated backup metadata to the owner's Google Drive. OAuth authorization credentials are stored in restricted files on the owner's server to enable unattended backups. The utility does not use Google Drive contents for advertising or profiling.

Sharing and third parties

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account using Google's hosting service. The utility does not sell or share Google user data with advertisers or other third parties.

Retention and deletion

Backup retention is controlled by the owner. The owner may delete files from Google Drive, revoke the utility's Google account access, or remove the backup configuration at any time.

Security

The intended backup process encrypts archive contents locally before upload. Access to OAuth credentials and backup encryption keys must be restricted by the owner. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For questions about this personal-use utility, contact the application owner using the support address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
